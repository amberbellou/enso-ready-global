Elche: rain close to normal is uncertain in Nov–Mar 2026-27.

That season starts in about 2 months (Nov–Mar 2026-27).

During El Niño, North Africa and the Mediterranean usually sees rain close to normal and temperatures close to normal.

ENSO has little consistent effect here. Local forecasts matter more. [iri_impacts]

There is no reliable local rain record for this season, so treat this as uncertain.

In the last 6 strong El Niño events, Nov–Mar rain here averaged 13% below normal. It was wetter than normal in 33% of them.

Most recent events: 2009-10: 56% above normal; 2015-16: 33% below normal; 2023-24: 43% below normal. [CHIRPS v2.0 monthly (UCSB Climate Hazards Center), 2° box average via IRI Data Library]
