Columbia: more rain than usual is likely in Nov–Mar 2026-27 (winter).

Main risks: flooding, storms and landslides.

That season starts in about 2 months (Nov–Mar 2026-27).

During El Niño, Southern United States and northern Mexico usually sees more rain than usual and cooler than usual.

A stronger jet stream steers more winter storms across California, the Southwest, Texas, the Gulf coast and the Southeast. [cpc_impacts]

The local rain record agrees with the known pattern, so this outlook is likely.

In the last 6 strong El Niño events, Nov–Mar rain here averaged 28% above normal. It was wetter than normal in 83% of them.

Most recent events: 2009-10: 57% above normal; 2015-16: 57% above normal; 2023-24: 36% above normal. [CHIRPS v2.0 monthly (UCSB Climate Hazards Center), 2° box average via IRI Data Library]

Global status: El Niño Advisory (issued 13 August 2026). [NOAA CPC]

What to do now

  1. 📄 Put ID cards, land papers and phone numbers in a sealed plastic bag. (about 15 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Papers are the hardest thing to replace after a flood.

  2. 🚶 Agree with your household on a high place to go and a way to get there. (about 20 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Deciding in the dark, in the rain, is when people get hurt.

  3. 💧 Store clean drinking water in closed containers, enough for three days. (about 30 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Floods pollute wells and taps first.

  4. 🧹 Clear drains, gutters and channels near your home now. (about 60 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Blocked drains turn heavy rain into a flood in your yard.

  5. 📦 Move food, seeds, fertiliser and valuables off the floor. (about 45 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Even shallow water ruins stored food and seed.

  6. 📻 Find out how your local warnings are given: radio, SMS, loudspeaker or a neighbour. (about 10 minutes)
    Why this matters

    The official warning is the signal to act.

  7. 💊 Keep a week of any daily medicines in the emergency bag. (about 10 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Clinics may be cut off for days.

  8. 🌳 Trim weak branches and secure loose items outdoors. (about 60 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Falling branches cause most storm damage to homes.

  9. 👀 Watch for new cracks, tilting trees or springs on slopes above your home. (about 10 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Slopes give warning signs before they fail.

  10. 🏠 If you live below a steep slope, arrange a safer place to sleep during heavy rain. (about 20 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Landslides mostly strike at night after days of rain.
