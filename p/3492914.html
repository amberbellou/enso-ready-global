Santiago de los Caballeros: less rain than usual is uncertain in Jun–Nov 2026.

Main risks: drought, water shortage and extreme heat.

That season is happening now (Jun–Nov 2026).

During El Niño, Caribbean islands usually sees less rain than usual and warmer than usual.

Drier wet season and usually fewer Atlantic hurricanes. [cpc_impacts]

Past events here were mixed, so treat this as uncertain.

In the last 6 strong El Niño events, Jun–Nov rain here averaged 9% above normal. It was wetter than normal in 67% of them.

Most recent events: 2009-10: 61% above normal; 2015-16: 26% below normal; 2023-24: 18% above normal. [CHIRPS v2.0 monthly (UCSB Climate Hazards Center), 2° box average via IRI Data Library]

Global status: El Niño Advisory (issued 13 August 2026). [NOAA CPC]

What to do now

  1. 💧 Fix leaks and add water storage: tanks, drums, or a covered pit. (about 120 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Every litre saved now is a litre you do not have to carry later.

  2. 🍚 Buy staple food a little at a time now while prices are normal. (about 30 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Food prices rise fastest when harvests fail.

  3. 👥 Agree with neighbours how a shared well or tank will be rationed. (about 45 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Agreed rules prevent conflict when water gets short.

  4. 💧 Store water and plan irrigation if you are in the north or east. (about 60 minutes)
    Why this matters

    El Niño summers are often dry there.

  5. 🚿 Reuse washing water for gardens and toilets. (about 10 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Cuts household water use by a third.

  6. 🌡️ Move hard work to early morning and late afternoon. (about 5 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Heat illness peaks in the middle of the day.

  7. 👵 Check daily on elderly neighbours and people living alone. (about 10 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Most heat deaths happen quietly at home.

  8. 💧 Drink water often, before you feel thirsty. (about 1 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Thirst comes late when the body is already short of water.

  9. 📢 Follow your national weather service for warnings; this briefing is a seasonal outlook, not a warning. (about 5 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Only official services issue alerts for specific days and places.

  10. 🗣️ Tell one neighbour what this season may bring. (about 5 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Communities that talk about risk early prepare better.
