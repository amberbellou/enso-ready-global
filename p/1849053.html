Utsunomiya: rain close to normal is uncertain in Dec–Aug 2026-27.

Main risks: a mild winter and a cool summer.

That season starts in about 3 months (Dec–Aug 2026-27).

During El Niño, Japan usually sees rain close to normal and a milder winter and cooler summer.

Mild winters and cool, wet summers are somewhat more common. [iri_impacts]

There is no reliable local rain record for this season, so treat this as uncertain.

In the last 6 strong El Niño events, Dec–Aug rain here averaged 21% above normal. It was wetter than normal in 83% of them.

Most recent events: 2009-10: 41% above normal; 2015-16: 12% below normal; 2023-24: 23% above normal. [CHIRPS v2.0 monthly (UCSB Climate Hazards Center), 2° box average via IRI Data Library]

Global status: El Niño Advisory (issued 13 August 2026). [NOAA CPC]
