Qarchak: more rain than usual is uncertain in Nov–Apr 2026-27 (the winter rains).

Main risks: flash floods and landslides.

That season starts in about 2 months (Nov–Apr 2026-27).

During El Niño, Iran, Iraq, Afghanistan, northern Pakistan and the Arabian Peninsula usually sees more rain than usual and temperatures close to normal.

Winter and spring rains tend to be heavier, sometimes causing flash floods in dry areas. [iri_impacts]

The local rain record does not clearly agree with the known pattern, so treat this as uncertain.

In the last 6 strong El Niño events, Nov–Apr rain here averaged 10% below normal. It was wetter than normal in 17% of them.

Most recent events: 2009-10: 18% above normal; 2015-16: 9% below normal; 2023-24: 25% below normal. [CHIRPS v2.0 monthly (UCSB Climate Hazards Center), 2° box average via IRI Data Library]

Global status: El Niño Advisory (issued 13 August 2026). [NOAA CPC]

What to do now

  1. ⚠️ Never cross a flooded road or dry riverbed during heavy rain. (about 5 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Most flash-flood deaths happen in vehicles and river crossings.

  2. 👀 Watch for new cracks, tilting trees or springs on slopes above your home. (about 10 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Slopes give warning signs before they fail.

  3. 🏠 If you live below a steep slope, arrange a safer place to sleep during heavy rain. (about 20 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Landslides mostly strike at night after days of rain.

  4. 📢 Follow your national weather service for warnings; this briefing is a seasonal outlook, not a warning. (about 5 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Only official services issue alerts for specific days and places.

  5. 🗣️ Tell one neighbour what this season may bring. (about 5 minutes)
    Why this matters

    Communities that talk about risk early prepare better.
