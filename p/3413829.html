Reykjavík: rain close to normal is uncertain in Dec–Feb 2026-27.

Main risks: a mild winter.

That season starts in about 3 months (Dec–Feb 2026-27).

During El Niño, Northern Canada and Greenland usually sees rain close to normal and warmer than usual.

Western and central Canada usually have milder winters. [cpc_impacts]

There is no reliable local rain record for this season, so treat this as uncertain.

In the last 6 strong El Niño events, Dec–Feb rain here averaged 7% below normal. It was wetter than normal in 33% of them.

Most recent events: 2009-10: 26% below normal; 2015-16: 7% below normal; 2023-24: 55% below normal. [GPCP v2.3 monthly (NOAA NCEI), interpolated to 2°]

Global status: El Niño Advisory (issued 13 August 2026). [NOAA CPC]
