# Test round protocol (Annex B.6)

Goal: 8–12 testers on at least 4 continents, including at least 3 neurodivergent testers (ADHD, dyslexia or autism), on their own phones, before the December launch. Remote, 20 minutes each, no recording of personal data.

## Recruit
- Friends and online communities (Annex C.1); ADHD/autism community groups for neurodivergent testers; one farmer, one coastal resident, one city resident if possible.
- Ask for: country, phone type, usual language, and whether they use a screen reader.

## Session script (read aloud, 20 minutes)
1. "Open this link on your phone: https://amberbellou.github.io/enso-ready-global/. Find the briefing for where you live and your first preparation step." Start a timer. **Pass: under 60 seconds.**
2. "In one sentence, what will the coming season be like where you live?" **Pass: matches the headline's direction and confidence word.**
3. "What is one thing you would do this week?" **Pass: names a step from the list.**
4. "Change the language to yours" (if not English). "Does anything read wrong or strangely?" Note every phrase.
5. "Open Settings and turn on Easy Read and larger text." **Pass: done without help.**
6. "Imagine it is night, your battery is low, and you are worried. Is anything on this screen confusing or frightening?" Note everything (Annex B.5 stress test).
7. Screen-reader users: repeat step 1 with the reader on; keyboard users: with keyboard only.

## Record (one row per tester, no names)
country · phone · language · time to first step (s) · steps 2–6 pass/fail · quotes · barriers

## After the round
- Any failure of step 1 or 6 is a release blocker.
- Translation notes go into the language's review sheet (`docs/REVIEW_KIT.md`).
- File the summary as `docs/test-rounds/<date>.md`.
