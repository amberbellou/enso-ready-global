# Terms and disclaimer

This page says what ENSO Ready Global is, what it is not, and what you agree to when you use it. It is written in plain words. Last updated: [date].

## What this service is

ENSO Ready Global is a seasonal outlook. It tells you what El Niño or La Niña has meant for your area in past events, and what the current season may bring. It also suggests preparation steps.

It is built from public climate data. Every number links to its source. No sentence is written freely by an AI. Each one is a fixed template filled with numbers.

It is free. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

## What this service is not

It is not a warning service. It cannot tell you what will happen on a given day or in a given week.

It is not an official service. It is not part of any government, the UN, WMO, NOAA or ECMWF. It uses their public data. It does not speak for them.

It is not advice for a specific decision. It is general information for an area about 220 km wide.

## Who to follow for warnings

For warnings and emergencies, follow your national meteorological service and your local authorities. Every briefing links to the service for your country. If their advice differs from this site, follow them.

## No guarantee

Seasonal forecasts are uncertain. Past events do not always repeat. Grid cells hide local differences between hills, coasts and cities. Data updates can fail, and the site may then show older data marked with its date. Translations may contain errors, and machine-translated pages say so.

For these reasons, the service is provided as it is, with no promise that it is accurate, complete or up to date.
