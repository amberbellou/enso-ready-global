# Accessibility

This page says how ENSO Ready works for people with disabilities. It also says what does not work well yet, and how to tell us about a problem.

ENSO Ready is a free website. It is built by one person. It is not an official warning service. For warnings, follow your national meteorological service.

## What we aim for

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 level AA. This is the common international standard for accessible websites.

We also follow the W3C guidance on cognitive accessibility (COGA). The site is meant to work for people with ADHD, dyslexia, autism, anxiety, or a learning disability. It is also meant to work for anyone who is tired, stressed, or on a slow phone.

## How the site is designed

- One thing per screen. One question, one answer, or one action.
- The answer comes first. Details come after, and you can open them if you want.
- Short sentences. Plain words. No idioms.
- One main button per screen, with a verb on it.
- Nothing moves or plays unless you ask for it. No pop-ups. No time limits.
- Risks are stated calmly. Each risk is followed by something you can do.
- Colour is never the only signal. Every warning has an icon and a word too.
- Buttons are at least 44 pixels tall.

## Settings you can change

Open **Settings** on the home screen. Your choices are saved on your device only.

- **Text size.** Three sizes.
- **High contrast.** Black text on white.
- **Easy Read.** Simpler words, one sentence per line, bigger icons.
- **Dyslexia-friendly spacing.** Wider letter and line spacing.
- **Reduce motion.** Turns off all animation. The site also follows the "reduce motion" setting on your phone or computer.

## Read aloud

Every briefing has a **Read aloud** button. It uses the voice built into your device. It sends nothing to us.

## Keyboard and screen readers

- Every button and link can be reached with the Tab key.
- A "Skip to main content" link appears when you press Tab on any page.
- Search results are announced as they appear.
- Settings buttons say whether they are on or off.
- The language you choose is set on the page, so screen readers use the right voice.

## Right-to-left languages

Arabic and other right-to-left scripts flip the layout. Text, borders, and buttons mirror to the right side.

## Known problems

We want to be honest about what is not finished.

- **Machine translations.** Most languages are translated by a machine. They may contain mistakes. Each of these pages shows a banner and a link to report a fix.
- **The map picker.** If you tap "Pick on a map", the map and its tiles come from third-party servers (OpenStreetMap and a map library host). We do not control how accessible the map is. You do not need the map. You can type a place name or use your phone's location instead.
- **Read aloud depends on your device.** Some devices have no voice for some languages. Voice quality varies.
- **Not yet tested with real users of assistive technology.** We have checked the site with a keyboard and with automated tools. We have not yet done a full test with screen reader users or with neurodivergent testers. This is planned before public launch. Until then, some barriers may exist that we do not know about.
- **Easy Read is not yet checked in every language.** The English version is complete. Other languages are being reviewed.
