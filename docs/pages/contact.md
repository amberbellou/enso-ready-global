# Contact and corrections

ENSO Ready is built and run by one person. There is no help desk. The best way to reach us is to open a report on GitHub. Every report is public, so anyone can see it and help.

**This page is not for emergencies.** If you need help now, contact your local emergency services or your national meteorological service.

## What you can report

- A fact that looks wrong. For example, a number, a season, or a hazard that does not match your area.
- A bad translation. A sentence that reads oddly, or means the wrong thing, in your language.
- A missing place. A town or village you cannot find in search.
- An accessibility barrier. Something you cannot read, reach, or use with your screen reader, keyboard, or phone.
- A broken link, a source that has moved, or a licence question.

You can also tell us what worked. That helps too.

## How to send a report

1. Open the corrections form: [Open a report on GitHub](https://github.com/amberbellou/enso-ready-global/issues/new).
2. Fill in the fields below.
3. Press "Submit new issue".

You need a free GitHub account to submit. If you do not have one and do not want one, ask someone who does to post it for you. Do not put your own address, phone number, or other personal details in the report.

### The corrections form

The form has four fields. The first three are needed. The last one is optional.

| Field | What to write |
|---|---|
| Place | The place name shown in your briefing, or the name you searched for. Country helps. |
| Language | The language you were reading in. |
| What is wrong | One or two sentences. Say what you saw and what you expected. |
| Source (if any) | A link or the name of a report that shows the correct fact. Leave blank if you have none. |

For a translation fix, the "Machine-translated" banner at the top of the briefing opens the same form with the language already filled in.

For an accessibility barrier, add what you were using. For example: "Android phone, TalkBack, Chrome". That helps us repeat the problem.

## What happens after you report

1. Your report appears in public on GitHub straight away.
2. The maintainer reads new reports [at least once a week].
3. We reply on the report. We may ask one or two questions.
4. If the report is right, we fix the data, the text, or the code. Fixed reports are marked "closed".
5. Fixes to text and data reach the live site with the next build. Builds run at least once a month, and sooner for a clear error.
6. A translation fix marks that sentence as reviewed. It will not be overwritten by the next machine translation.

We do not promise a fix by a set date. This is a volunteer project.

## What we do not collect

- No email address. There is no sign-up box on this site.
- No name or location, unless you choose to type it into a report.
- No cookies and no tracking on this page or any other.

Reports live on GitHub and follow GitHub's own terms and privacy policy. Do not write anything in a report that you would not want to be public.
